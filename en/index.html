02 / How I work

FROM THE BRIEF
TO FINAL DELIVERY.

A straightforward process built around clear decisions and finished files that are ready to use.

  1. 01

    The brief

    You send me the goal, product, script and any references. If the direction is still open, I help shape it with clear examples.

  2. 02

    Shot plan

    I map out the shots, framing, supporting footage and production order needed to make the idea work.

  3. 03

    Production

    I film, shoot the photographs or step in front of the camera—whatever the project calls for.

  4. 04

    Final delivery

    I turn the material into polished, platform-ready files in the formats we agreed on.
